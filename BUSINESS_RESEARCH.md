## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Essence

Francis Bean is a beloved, independent beachside café located at 3 Mari Street in the coastal enclave of Alexandra Headland on Queensland’s Sunshine Coast. Positioned just moments from the Alexandra Headland patrolled surf beach and coastal promenade, the café caters to local surfers, morning walkers, young families, holidaymakers, and specialty coffee lovers.

The café has cultivated a warm, relaxed, unpretentious coastal culture paired with high standards for coffee—proudly serving Sydney-founded **Campos Coffee** (renowned for its caramel and sweet chocolate-noted *Superior Blend*). Its breakfast and brunch offering bridges the gap between quick grab-and-go morning nourishment (artisan wraps, toasted bagels, bacon & egg rolls) and seated brunch classics (chilli scrambled eggs, eggs Benedict variations, vibrant açaí bowls).

Visually, Francis Bean expresses an approachable, playful, hand-crafted personality. Its physical and print materials—evidenced by the attached illustrated menu—use warm peach-toned textured paper, earthy cocoa typography, friendly hand-drawn illustrations, cute character badges (such as a smiling fried egg and cheerful sunshine biscuit), and vintage editorial warmth.

## 4. Dietary & Allergen Safety Directives

1. **Vegetarian Options:** Clearly present in items such as Smash Avo on Toast, Halloumi Bagel, Eggs on Toast, Açaí Bowl, and Greek Yoghurt Bowl.
2. **Gluten-Free Availability:** Gluten-free bread substitution may be available in-store upon request, but the kitchen is an active bakery environment handling regular wheat flour.
3. **Severe Allergies Policy:** Under no circumstances should the digital chatbot or frontend copy guarantee an item is completely allergy-free or cross-contamination free. Chatbot and copy must always prompt customers with serious nut, dairy, or coeliac conditions to speak directly with the kitchen staff by phone (`07 5479 0079`) or in person.
